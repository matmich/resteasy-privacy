  • Supabase — hosting of the database, authentication and photos (servers in the European Union, Ireland region).
  • Expo (Expo Push Service) — delivery of push notifications.
  • Infomaniak — sending of invitation and password-reset emails (Switzerland).

We do not sell or rent your data to third parties.

4. Retention

Restaurant data is kept as long as the administrator account exists, so that the hygiene register is preserved. When an employee is removed or deletes their account, their personal data (email, password, notification token) is deleted; their name remains attached to the readings they logged, to preserve the integrity of the hygiene register. When the administrator deletes their account, the restaurant and all of its data are permanently deleted.

5. Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the GDPR, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your data. You can delete your account directly in the app: Settings → Delete my account. For any other request: matmich.121@gmail.com.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access is separated per restaurant and limited by role (administrator / manager / employee) and by location at the database level; personal and HR details are not visible to colleagues. Temperature logs cannot be edited once recorded.

7. Children

RestEasy is intended for professional use and is not directed at children.
